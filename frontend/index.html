Common questions

Is my data safe?

Your files are read in memory to check your claim and are not saved to disk. There is no login and no account.

What if the insurer was actually right?

We'll tell you plainly. If the rejection follows your policy and IRDAI rules, you'll see "Valid rejection" along with what you can still do, like when the waiting period ends.

Where do I send the appeal letter?

To your insurer's Grievance Redressal Officer. The email address is usually in your rejection letter or on the insurer's website. They must reply within 30 days. If they don't, you can go to IRDAI's Bima Bharosa portal and then the Insurance Ombudsman, which is free.

Is this legal advice?

No. ClaimSathi helps you understand your rejection and draft a letter. For large or complex claims, consider speaking to a lawyer or the Insurance Ombudsman.
